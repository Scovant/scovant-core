10 Tips for Better Running

By Jane Doe | January 15, 2024

1. Warm Up Properly

Always start with a 5-minute warm-up walk or light jog to prepare your muscles.

2. Maintain Good Form

Keep your shoulders relaxed and arms at 90 degrees. Look ahead, not down.

3. Breathe Rhythmically

Try a 2:2 breathing pattern — inhale for 2 steps, exhale for 2 steps.

Advanced Breathing Techniques

Some elite runners use belly breathing to maximize oxygen intake.

4. Stay Hydrated

Drink water before, during (for runs over 30 min), and after your run.
